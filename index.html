Rosters, injury tags and season averages load live from ESPN’s public data each time you pick a team, then stay cached on this device for a few hours (use Refresh rosters to pull them again). Early in a season, a player’s ratings blend his few current-season games with last season. Players marked Out are left out automatically; untick anyone else you want to sit.
